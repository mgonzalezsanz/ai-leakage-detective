# Credit Memo Policy

## When to issue
A credit memo reduces what a customer owes and should be issued when an invoice was overbilled — most commonly due to a pricing error or a currency conversion mistake (see `fx_conversion_policy.md`). Always check for an existing credit memo on the invoice before proposing a new one; never propose a duplicate fix for an issue that's already resolved.

## Required documentation
Every credit memo must cite: the original invoice ID, the corrected amount, and a specific reason (e.g. "FX overbilling adjustment (EUR→USD)"). Vague reasons like "billing error" are not sufficient for audit purposes.

## Approval thresholds
Credit memos under $15,000 can be applied with standard customer confirmation. At or above $15,000, see `escalation_thresholds.md` — account-manager sign-off is required before applying.
